1. Introduction

JerkyCandy ("we", "us") operates this website and develops the game FOURVIVE. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

Website

  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us by email (such as your email address and message content).

  • Platform account identifiers and display names (via Steam, Epic Online Services including guest sign-in on Android where offered, STOVE on PC where applicable, and/or Google Play–related identifiers on Android).

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate and maintain this website.
  • Provide, operate, and improve FOURVIVE and its online features.
  • Enable multiplayer connectivity, matchmaking, and co-op sessions.
  • Save and sync your game data across devices.
  • Display leaderboards, rankings, and achievements.

For account and data deletion steps, see account-delete.html.

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.
