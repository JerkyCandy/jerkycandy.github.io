Last updated: June 29, 2026

App FOURVIVE
Developer JerkyCandy

How to request account and data deletion

  1. Remove local app data on your device

    Uninstall FOURVIVE from your device, or open your device settings and clear the app's storage (app data). This removes save files, settings, and other data stored locally on your device.

  2. Delete or manage your Epic Games account (if applicable)

    FOURVIVE signs you in through Epic Games (Epic Online Services / OAuth). JerkyCandy does not create or operate a separate game account. To delete your Epic Games account and any data held by Epic, follow Epic's account deletion process: Epic Games Help — Delete your account

  3. Contact us if you need assistance

    If you have questions about data associated with FOURVIVE, email us at jerkycandydev@gmail.com or jerkycandy@naver.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

About accounts in FOURVIVE

FOURVIVE does not let you create a separate JerkyCandy account and does not store user account databases on JerkyCandy's own servers.

Sign-in is handled through Epic Games (Epic Online Services) using OAuth. Your Epic Games account is managed by Epic Games, not by JerkyCandy.

Data deleted vs. retained

Data type What happens when you delete Additional retention
Local app data (save files, settings, customization, cache) Deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data in device settings. None on your device after uninstall or app data clear. Deletion is immediate.
Epic Games account information (account ID, display name, authentication tokens) Managed by Epic Games. Delete or manage through your Epic account settings. Epic may retain data according to its own policies and legal requirements. See Epic's privacy policy for details.
Online features via Epic Online Services (e.g., multiplayer sessions, lobbies, leaderboards) Session data ends when you leave a session. Persistent platform-side records, if any, are handled by Epic Online Services. JerkyCandy does not operate separate servers for these features. Any retention follows Epic's terms and policies.
Support emails you send to JerkyCandy Deleted upon request. Contact us using the email addresses above. Kept only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, unless a longer period is required by law.
Because FOURVIVE does not maintain its own user account system, there is no in-app "delete account" button operated by JerkyCandy. Removing local app data and managing your Epic Games account are the steps that apply.

Third-party services

FOURVIVE on Android uses Epic Online Services for sign-in (OAuth). Epic Games processes account and platform-related data under its own privacy policy:

Epic Games Privacy Policy

Other platforms (such as Steam on PC) use their own account systems. Account deletion on those platforms is managed by the respective provider.
